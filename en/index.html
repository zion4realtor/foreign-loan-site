Frequently asked questions

Can I get a U.S. mortgage without a green card or SSN?

Yes. Foreign national loan programs typically use your passport and visa/entry records and do not require a U.S. credit history, but down payments are usually higher and documentation differs.

How much down payment is typical for a foreign buyer?

It varies by program and profile. Foreign buyer programs commonly require 25%–40% down; borrowers with U.S. income and credit history may qualify for conventional programs with lower down payments.

Can I do this remotely from overseas?

Most steps can be completed remotely, including pre-approval, document submission and most signing; closing requirements vary by state and title company. Service covers Georgia and California.
